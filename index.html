Snapdeal Analytics – Power BI

Data Analytics Internship Project

About the Project

This project was completed as part of my Data Analytics internship. The project uses Snapdeal product data to study pricing, discounts, ratings, reviews, promotions and inventory-related risk.

The analysis was carried out using Power BI, Power Query and DAX.

Tasks Completed

Task 1 – Pricing vs Satisfaction

Compared product prices, ratings and estimated sales and created measures to identify products that may require attention.

Task 2 – Promotion Effectiveness

Compared promotional and non-promotional discounts. The baseline discount was 14.67% and the promotion discount was 69.65%.

Task 3 – Discount vs Rating

Studied the relationship between discount, rating and review volume. The regression slope was approximately 0.01.

Task 4 – Financial Risk

Created a financial risk KPI. The calculated financially at-risk inventory value was 25.40%.

Task 5 – Trust-Weighted Rating

Compared simple average, review-weighted and trust-weighted ratings. The trust-weighted rating was 4.11.

Task 6 – Dynamic Price Banding

Created Low, Medium and High price bands using the 30th and 70th percentiles.

Tools Used

Power BI   |   Power Query   |   DAX   |   CSV

Limitations

The dataset was a product-level snapshot and did not contain enough historical information for a proper 30-day trend analysis.

Actual sales, complete inventory history and actual return-rate data were also not available. Therefore, some of the required metrics were calculated using available fields as estimates or proxies.

Because of these limitations, the discount and rating analysis shows relationships in the available data and does not prove causation.
